Signature Dish

Classic Sticky Toffee Pudding

A rich, dark sponge cake made with finely chopped dates, covered in a luscious, buttery toffee sauce.

Ingredients

For the Pudding

  • 1 ¾ cups (225g) Medjool dates, pitted and chopped
  • ¾ cup (180ml) boiling water
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ¾ cup (175g) unsalted butter, softened
  • ¾ cup (150g) dark brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 ¾ cups (225g) all-purpose flour
  • 1 ½ tsp baking powder

For the Toffee Sauce

  • 1 cup (200g) dark brown sugar
  • ½ cup (115g) unsalted butter
  • 1 cup (240ml) heavy cream
  • ½ tsp sea salt

Method of Preparation

  1. Prepare the Dates: Place the chopped dates in a bowl and pour the boiling water over them. Stir in the baking soda and let them sit for 10 minutes to soften. Mash slightly with a fork.
  2. Cream Butter and Sugar: In a large bowl, cream the butter and dark brown sugar until light and fluffy, then beat in the eggs and vanilla.
  3. Combine: Fold in the flour and baking powder, then stir in the date mixture (including the liquid) to create the batter.
  4. Bake: Pour the thin batter into a greased baking dish and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 30–35 minutes.
  5. Make the Sauce: While the cake bakes, combine brown sugar, butter, and cream in a saucepan and simmer until thickened and glossy.
  6. The "Sticky" Finish: Poke holes in the warm pudding and pour half of the warm toffee sauce over the top to soak in.
  7. Serve: Serve warm with the remaining sauce drizzled over each portion, topped with vanilla ice cream or heavy cream.
